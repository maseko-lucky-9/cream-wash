## Design Context

### Users
Prospective franchise owners evaluating Cream Wash as a business opportunity. They're viewing a live demo on a phone or laptop, likely in a meeting or pitch setting. The presenter (likely the founder) is narrating the experience. First impressions are the product — clunkiness directly undermines the premium pitch.

### Brand Personality
Premium, warm, local. Fourways, Gauteng — aspirational but not cold. Quality you can feel, priced for the South African market, rooted in the neighbourhood.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm cream/amber palette on a light background. Glassmorphism used sparingly and purposefully — not as decoration but for layering depth. The design should feel like the lobby of a good car dealership, not a startup landing page. Fonts: DM Sans (display), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (PIN). Touch targets 56px for primary staff actions, 48px general.

### Design Principles
1. **Demo-first UX**: Every interaction should flow without the presenter touching the keyboard. Navigation between scenarios must be frictionless.
2. **Earned premium**: Quality over decoration. Avoid anything that looks designed to look premium — just be precise with spacing, type, and colour.
3. **Local warmth**: Amber and cream feel like morning sun on stone. Keep the palette grounded, never icy or clinical.
4. **One interaction at a time**: The booking flow is a funnel — remove everything that doesn't serve the current step.
5. **Data tells the story**: The owner dashboard exists to make a single argument: "you can see your business at a glance." Every element should reinforce that claim.
